# Nat Home Made Curry Puff (Balik Pulau, Penang)

A bespoke, responsive, artisanal website designed and crafted for **Nat Home Made Curry Puff**, located in the scenic town of Balik Pulau on Penang Island.

---

## 🌟 Brand Story & Core Highlights

> *"In the scenic town of Balik Pulau on Penang Island, we craft traditional Karipap Pusing (spiral-layered curry puffs) made with 100% passion, fresh ingredients, and time-honored Asian pastry techniques."*

### The 4 Golden Pillars of Nat's Curry Puff:
1. **🥔 100% Farm-Fresh Local Potatoes (No Meat / Vegetarian-Friendly):**
   - Premium Russet potatoes sourced directly from farms.
   - Hand-peeled and diced into uniform 5mm cubes daily so they cook up soft, fluffy, and aromatic inside without turning mushy.
2. **🌿 Aromatic Malaysian Spices & Curry Leaves ("Pecah Minyak"):**
   - No artificial flavorings, MSG cubes, or meat fillers.
   - Fresh shallots, garlic, grated ginger, and garden-picked curry leaves sautéed in pure oil.
   - Blooming roasted cumin, coriander, fennel, and authentic Malaysian curry powder.
3. **🌀 Shatteringly Flaky "Karipap Pusing" Spiral Crust:**
   - Signature double-dough rolling technique (alternating Water Dough + Oil Dough).
   - Micro-layers bloom into golden, ultra-crispy spiral rings.
   - **Guaranteed crunchy for up to 8 hours!**
4. **🍳 Fried Fresh Daily at Precisely 170°C:**
   - Never sold frozen or pre-fried.
   - Hand-crimped (*Tali Pilin*) and flash-fried in small batches at precisely 170°C for a light, grease-free, piping hot experience.

## 🚀 Key Features of the Website

- **Artisanal Heritage Aesthetics:** Warm turmeric amber, terracotta spices, and deep botanical curry leaf green color palette.
- **Interactive Fresh Batch Countdown Timer:** Live ticker showing when the next fresh batch leaves the 170°C oil.
- **Acoustic Pastry Crunch Synthesizer:** Built-in Web Audio API sound engine that generates a hyper-crispy multi-layer crackle effect on click (*"Hear The Crunch"* button).
- **Interactive Spiral Anatomy Visualizer:** Explorable cross-section of the Karipap Pusing showing the double-dough layers, uniform 5mm Russet potato core, and hand-crimped rope braid.
- **Honest Comparison Table:** Contrasts Nat's handcrafted spiral puffs against factory frozen puffs.
- **Dynamic Box Pre-Order Calculator:**
  - Crisp Taster Box (5 Pcs - RM 15.00)
  - Family Sharing Box (10 Pcs - RM 28.00 - *Most Popular*)
  - Gathering & Gift Box (20 Pcs - RM 52.00)
  - Live quantity adjustment with instant automatic bundle savings calculation.
- **Direct WhatsApp Order Generator:** Pre-formats customer name, pickup slot in Balik Pulau, quantity, and total for 1-click booking on WhatsApp.
- **Air Fryer & Oven Reheat Guide:** Exact temperature and timing instructions to restore 100% crunch.
- **Balik Pulau Pickup & Scenic Route Guide:** Interactive 1-click address copy for Waze and Google Maps, operating hours, and travel times from George Town and Penang Airport.
- **Interactive FAQ Accordion:** Answers dietary questions (vegetarian-friendly, meat-free), batch timings, and island delivery.
